Common questions

My finger doesn't draw anything.
Settings has a “Draw with Apple Pencil only” switch. If it is on, a finger can only scroll and tap. Turn it off to draw with a finger again.
I bought the advanced set and it isn't unlocked.
Open Settings inside the app and tap “Restore purchase”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
Does it work offline?
Yes, entirely. The app never needs a network connection except when you buy or restore the in-app purchase.
Where are my drawings stored?
Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, and there is no cloud copy — see the privacy policy.
The app says it isn't sure what I drew.
In Doodle Challenge the app names one of its 109 beginner subjects, you draw it from memory in 20 seconds, and it tries to recognise it among those same subjects — all on your device, with nothing sent anywhere. When it cannot tell, it says so rather than guessing.

常见问题

手指画不出线。
设置里有「只用 Apple Pencil 作画」的开关。开启后手指只能滚动和点按。关掉它就能用手指画了。
买了完整题库但没有解锁。
打开 app 内的设置，点「恢复购买」。请确认登录的是当初购买时用的那个 Apple 账号。
能离线用吗？
完全可以。除了购买或恢复内购那一刻，app 全程不需要联网。
我的画存在哪里？
只存在你的设备上。删除 app 就会一并删除，没有云端副本——详见隐私政策。
app 说认不出我画的是什么。
猜画挑战会从 app 自带的 109 张入门题里报一个题目，你用 20 秒凭记忆画出来，它再在这些题目里认你画的是什么； 全程在本机进行，什么都不上传。认不出来时它会直接说认不出，而不是硬猜。
