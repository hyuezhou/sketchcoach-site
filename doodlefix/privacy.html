Privacy Policy

DoodleFix (人人漫画家) · Last updated 2 October 2026

DoodleFix has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your drawings stay on your device. They leave it only when you start one of the app's AI features — AI Fix, Describe it or Circle a layout — or when you share a picture yourself. An AI request goes to our server and then to Google's Gemini API to generate a result, and we keep none of what you sent; the only thing our server keeps is a daily request count, described below.

What we collect

There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app talks to two things and nothing else: Apple's App Store for purchases, and our AI server when — and only when — you start one of the AI features. What each request contains is described below. We keep no copy of your pictures or words and cannot connect a request to you.

What stays on your device

Your drawings and every earlier round of them, photos you import, your settings and your remaining AI fixes are written to your device's own storage. The drawings also appear in the Files app, and they are part of any iPhone or iPad backup you make yourself; DoodleFix keeps no copy anywhere else. What you copy with the Copy tool, to paste later, is kept only in the app's cache on the device (not on the system clipboard) and is never uploaded. Everything is removed when you delete the app — all but one small marker in the device's keychain that records that the two included fixes were given, so a reinstall does not give them again. Tools that work on the picture itself — “Fill”, “Spray”, “Ink brush”, “Stay-in-the-lines brush”, “Erase background”, “Text”, “Copy”, “Paste” and “Extract lines” — run entirely on the device.

Network connections

Two things in the app use the network, and nothing else does.

Purchases. Buying a pack of AI fixes goes through Apple's own App Store purchase flow, handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We receive no information about you from it — not your name, not your Apple Account, not your payment details.

The AI features (optional). When you choose to use one, this is sent to our server (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to Google's Gemini API and returns the result to your device. The server does not keep what you sent.

  • AI Fix — a rectangle of your drawing around the part you circled, with your circle marked on it (and a mask of the circle); a small copy of the whole picture, used only to match your style; whether you chose Fix, Add or Replace; and your hint. In whole-picture mode, the whole picture (at most 1024 pixels on its longer side), the “Strength” setting and your hint. “3 versions” sends it three times, each asking for a different take.
  • Describe it — the sentence you typed and the style you picked, with blank paper. Nothing you have drawn is sent.
  • Circle a layout — the outlines you drew, their numbers and your notes, and the style you picked, on blank paper. Nothing you have already drawn is sent.

Every request also includes the app's language and name, and AI Fix also sends the app's guess of whether the drawing is line art or a sketch. We do not store any of it, do not link it to you and do not use it for anything else. Google processes it to produce the result; under the Gemini API terms that apply to our paid use, Google does not use it to train its models and may retain it only briefly for abuse detection.

Each request carries a random identifier created when the app is installed (a new one after a reinstall). The server keeps only a count of that identifier's requests for the day, deleted automatically within 36 hours, so it can cap requests per install. Your IP address is used, and not stored by us, to limit how often requests can come from one network. Neither is tied to your name, Apple Account or device. Before the first upload, the app explains what is sent and how it is handled, and none of these features runs unless you tap to start it.

Children

The app is rated 4+. There is no chat, no user-to-user content and no advertising; Settings has three links out of the app: this privacy policy, the help page, and our other app, SketchTutor, on the App Store. The only way DoodleFix itself sends a child's drawing or photo off the device is one of the AI features above: they never run on their own, the app asks for consent before the first use, and the consent screen asks children under 13 to check with a parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child has sent us something, write to us and we will help.

Saving and sharing a drawing

“Save to Photos” adds the picture to your photo library, with your permission. The share button hands an image to the iOS share sheet, under your control; where it goes is decided by you and governed by whichever app you send it to. Nothing is uploaded by DoodleFix.

隐私政策

人人漫画家（DoodleFix）· 最后更新 2026 年 10 月 2 日

本 app 没有账号、没有统计、没有广告，你的画只存在你的设备上。只有当你主动使用 AI 功能（「AI 改一改」「说一句」「圈出布局」）并点下开始时，或者你自己分享图片时，画才会离开设备。AI 请求经我们的服务器交给 Google 的 Gemini API 生成结果；除了下文说的每日请求计数，我们什么都不保留。

我们收集什么

没有账号，不需要登录，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有任何第三方 SDK。app 只和两处通信：购买时的 Apple App Store，以及你开始使用 AI 功能时我们的 AI 服务器。每种请求里有什么，下面逐一写明；我们不保留你的画和文字的副本，也无法把请求和你对应起来。

什么留在你的设备上

你的画及其之前的每一轮、你导入的照片、设置、剩余的 AI 次数，都写在你自己设备的存储里。画在「文件」app 里也能看到，也会包含在你自己做的 iPhone 或 iPad 备份里；人人漫画家不在别处保存副本。用「复制」复制、留着以后粘贴的内容，只存在本机的 app 缓存里（不放进系统剪贴板），不会上传。删除 app 时它们一并删除——只有设备钥匙串里的一个小标记会留下，记着附带的 2 次已经给过，重装时不会再给一次。涂色桶、喷枪、水墨毛笔、不出线笔、擦底图、文字、复制和粘贴，以及「提取线稿」这些直接处理画面的工具，全部在设备上完成。

联网的两处

app 里只有两件事会联网，其余一律不联网。

购买。购买 AI 次数包走的是 Apple 自己的购买流程，完全由 Apple 的 StoreKit 处理。我们不会从中得到关于你的任何信息——姓名、Apple 账号、支付信息，都不会。

AI 功能（可选）。当你选择使用时，以下内容会传到我们的服务器（Cloudflare Worker），由它转给 Google 的 Gemini API，再把结果送回你的设备。服务器不保留你发送的内容。

  • AI 改一改——圈住那块周围的一块长方形画面（上面标着你画的圈，另附一张圈的遮罩）、一张缩小的整图（只用来对齐画风）、你选的「改好／加上／换成」和提示；「整张」模式是整张画（长边最大 1024 像素）、「改动幅度」和提示。「出 3 张」会把这个请求发三次，每次要一种不同的画法。
  • 说一句——你写的那句话和选的风格，配一张白纸，不发送任何画。
  • 圈出布局——你画的圈、编号和标注，以及选的风格，画在白纸上；不发送你已有的画。

每个请求还会带上 app 的语言设置和 app 名称；「AI 改一改」还会带上 app 对画风的判断（线稿还是素描）。我们不会存储这些内容，不会把它们和你关联，也不会用于任何别的用途。Google 处理它是为了生成结果；按我们付费使用的 Gemini API 条款，Google 不会用它训练模型，只可能为防滥用短暂保留。

每个请求带一个安装时随机生成的标识（重装后会换新）。服务器只按这个标识记一个当天的请求次数，36 小时内自动删除，用来限制每次安装每天的请求量；你的 IP 地址会用于限制同一网络的请求频率，我们不保存。这些都不和你的姓名、Apple 账号或设备挂钩。第一次上传前 app 会说明发送什么、如何处理；你不点开始，这些功能永远不会联网。

儿童

本 app 分级为 4+。app 内没有聊天、没有用户之间的内容、没有广告；设置里有三个外部链接：本隐私政策、帮助页，以及我们另一个 app「简笔画家」的 App Store 页面。本 app 自己把孩子的画或照片传出设备的唯一途径，是上面说的 AI 功能：它们不会自己运行，第一次使用前会先征求同意，同意页里也提醒未满 13 岁的孩子先问家长。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息；如果你认为孩子发来了什么，写信给我们，我们会协助处理。

保存和分享作品

「存到相册」会在你允许后把图片存进你的照片图库。分享按钮把一张图片交给 iOS 的系统分享面板，由你决定发到哪里，之后遵循你所选 app 的规则。人人漫画家本身不上传任何东西。

政策变更
