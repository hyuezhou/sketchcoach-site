DoodleFix — Support

Draw with AI: sketch it, then let the AI help.

Email info@astralogy.org and you will get a reply. Please say which iPhone or iPad you are using and which iOS version, and describe what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

On iPad, my finger doesn't draw anything.
Your iPad is set so that only the Apple Pencil draws and a finger scrolls. Turn on “Draw with Finger” in the tool palette's ··· menu, or turn off “Only Draw with Apple Pencil” in the Settings app under Apple Pencil. On iPhone a finger always draws.
What does the eraser take off?
What you drew and painted: pen strokes, and the colour from “Fill”, “Spray”, “Ink brush” and “Stay-in-the-lines brush”, back to the picture the round started with — the AI's picture, a photo, or white paper. The AI's picture or a photo itself is the background: “Erase background” takes it back to paper. When the eraser takes nothing off, the app says so, and “Switch to Erase background” changes over to it. The eraser's smallest size is fixed by the system; zoom in to erase finer.
How do I zoom in?
Pinch with two fingers: up to ten times the whole-page view on iPhone. Every tool works zoomed in, and two fingers move the page.
How do I add words or a speech bubble?
Pick “Text” in the tool palette and tap where the words should go. Type them, choose plain text or “Bubble” and a size on the card, drag the words on the page to move them, then tap “Done”.
Can I copy part of a picture?
Pick “Copy” and draw a loop around the part you want. The copy appears just beside it: drag it into place, size it with the slider, and leave “Remove the paper” on to take out the paper colour around it; then tap “Paste”. To paste it again, into this drawing or another one, use Paste in the ⋯ menu. The copy stays on your device.
How does the stay-in-the-lines brush work?
Pick “Stay-in-the-lines brush” and paint: the colour stays inside the part where the stroke started and stops at the lines by itself. A stroke that starts on a line can't paint; start a little farther inside.
Can I move or delete text, a speech bubble or a sticker after placing it?
Yes. Pick “Text” and tap the text or bubble on the page (“Copy” and a tap for a sticker): it comes back as you left it, to move, change, or delete with the trash button. “Put back” puts it back where it was. Anything you painted over it since stays. Text and bubbles can be picked up again even after the app was closed; stickers until then. The eraser rubs them out too.
I went back to the gallery by mistake.
Open the same drawing again: its undo history is still there, as long as the app hasn't been closed and it is one of the last three drawings you opened.
What happens to a blank drawing?
If you go back without drawing anything, the blank drawing isn't kept in the gallery. One you drew on and undid back to blank is kept.
What does “Strength” do in AI Fix?
In “Whole picture” mode, “Strength” — Light, Medium or Strong — sets how much the AI may change the picture.
What uses an AI fix?
Each “Fix it” (on a circled part or the whole picture), each “Try again”, each “Describe it” and each “Circle a layout” uses one; “3 versions” and “3 more” use three. A request that doesn't come out isn't counted. “Extract lines” and all the drawing tools work on your device and use nothing.
Does it work offline?
Drawing, the tools, the history of rounds and the gallery all work offline. Two things need a connection: buying a pack, and the AI features, which send what you chose to our server to be drawn (see the privacy policy).
Do the AI fixes carry over to another device, or to SketchTutor?
No. The two included fixes and any packs you buy are counted on the device they were used or bought on, and DoodleFix and SketchTutor count them separately. Unused fixes are lost if you delete the app, and the two included fixes are not given again on the same device.
Where are my drawings stored?
Only on your device (they also appear in the Files app). DoodleFix keeps no copy anywhere else; deleting the app deletes them, apart from any iPhone or iPad backup you make yourself — see the privacy policy.

常见问题

在 iPad 上手指画不出线。
这时只有 Apple Pencil 能画，手指用来滚动。在工具栏的 ··· 菜单里打开「用手指绘图」，或者在「设置」app 的 Apple Pencil 里关掉「仅使用 Apple Pencil 绘图」。iPhone 上手指总是能画。
橡皮擦能擦掉什么？
你画的和涂的：笔迹，以及涂色桶、喷枪、水墨毛笔、不出线笔涂上的颜色，擦回这一轮开始时的样子——AI 的画、照片，或者白纸。AI 的画和照片本身是底图，要用「擦底图」擦成白纸。橡皮擦什么都没擦掉时，app 会提示，点「用擦底图」就能换过去。橡皮擦最小的尺寸是系统定的，想擦得更细就放大画面。
怎么放大？
两根手指捏合，iPhone 上最多放大到整页视图的 10 倍。放大后所有工具照常用，两根手指可以移动画面。
怎么加文字或对话气泡？
在工具栏选「文字」，点一下要放字的地方。输入后在卡片上选普通文字还是「气泡」、选大小，拖动画面上的字可以挪位置，最后点「完成」。
能复制画里的一部分吗？
选「复制」，圈住想要的那一块，复制出来的会出现在旁边：拖动摆放，用滑杆调大小，「去掉纸色」开着就会去掉它周围的纸色，然后点「贴上」。想再贴到这张或别的画上，用 ⋯ 菜单里的「粘贴」。复制的内容只留在你的设备上。
不出线笔怎么用？
选「不出线笔」直接涂：从哪一块下笔，就只涂在那一块里，碰到线条自动停。从线上起笔涂不了，往里一点再画。
放好的字、气泡或贴纸还能挪、能删吗？
能。选「文字」点一下画上的字或气泡（贴纸用「复制」点），它会回到可以编辑的状态：可以挪、改字，或点垃圾桶删掉；点「放回去」放回原处。之后在它上面涂过的颜色会保留。字和气泡在 app 关掉再打开后也能拿起来，贴纸只在 app 没关时可以；也可以直接用橡皮擦擦掉。
不小心返回了画廊怎么办？
再打开同一张画，撤销记录还在（app 没被关掉、且是最近打开的 3 张之一时有效）。
什么都没画就返回了会怎样？
这张空白画不会留在画廊里。画过、又撤销回空白的画会保留。
AI 改一改里的「改动幅度」是什么？
「整张」模式下可以选「改动幅度」：小幅、中等、大幅，决定 AI 能改多少。
什么会用掉 AI 次数？
每次「改一改」（圈住的或整张）、「再来一次」、「说一句」、「圈出布局」各用 1 次；「出 3 张」「再出 3 张」用 3 次。没出来的不扣。「提取线稿」和所有画画工具都在设备上完成，不用次数。
能离线用吗？
画画、各种工具、历史轮次和画廊都不需要联网。只有两处要联网：购买次数包，以及 AI 功能——它会把你选的内容传到我们的服务器来画（见隐私政策）。
AI 次数能带到另一台设备，或者和简笔画家共用吗？
不能。附带的 2 次和购买的次数包都记在使用或购买它们的那台设备上；人人漫画家和简笔画家的次数也分开计算。删除 app 后，没用完的次数不会恢复；同一台设备重装也不会再给附带的 2 次。
我的画存在哪里？
只存在你的设备上（在「文件」app 里也能看到）。人人漫画家不在别处保存副本；删除 app 就会一并删除，你自己做的设备备份除外——详见 隐私政策。
