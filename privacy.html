Privacy Policy

SketchCoach · Last updated 7 September 2026

SketchCoach collects no personal data. The only thing it ever transmits is the piece of a drawing you choose to send to AI Fix, and that is discarded once the result comes back.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It contains no networking code of its own.

What stays on your device

Your drawings, your progress, and your settings are written to your device's own storage and never leave it. They are removed when you delete the app. Nothing is backed up to us, because there is no “us” to back up to — we operate no server.

Network connections

Two things in the app use the network, and nothing else does.

Purchases. Apple's own App Store purchase and restore flow runs when you buy or restore an in-app purchase. That is handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We receive no information about you from it — not your name, not your Apple Account, not your payment details.

AI Fix (optional, from version 1.2). When you choose to use AI Fix, the part of your drawing that you circled — or, if you choose the whole-picture mode, the whole picture — is sent to our server together with the short hint you typed, so that an AI image model can redraw it. The server forwards it to the model provider, returns the result to your device, and keeps nothing: the image is not stored, not linked to you, not used for training and not used for any other purpose. The request carries a random per-install identifier used only to limit how many fixes an install can make per day; it is not tied to your name, Apple Account or device identity. You are told this in the app before the first upload, and AI Fix never runs unless you tap "Fix it". Everything else — your drawings, your gallery, your progress — stays on your device exactly as before.

Children

The app is suitable for children. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. There is no chat, no user-to-user content, no external links, and no advertising.

Sharing a drawing

The share button hands an image to the iOS share sheet, under your control. Where that image goes is decided by you and governed by whichever app you send it to. Nothing is uploaded by SketchCoach.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: info@astralogy.org

隐私政策

简笔画家（SketchCoach）· 最后更新 2026 年 9 月 7 日

本 app 不收集任何个人数据。它唯一会传输的，是你主动交给「AI 改一改」的那一块画，结果一回来就丢弃。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。没有账号，不需要登录，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有任何第三方 SDK。 app 本身不含任何联网代码。

什么留在你的设备上

你的画、进度和设置都写在你自己设备的存储里，不会离开设备。删除 app 时它们一并删除。 我们不做任何备份——因为我们没有服务器。

联网的两处

app 里只有两件事会联网，其余一律不联网。

购买。你购买或恢复内购时，走的是 Apple 自己的购买流程，完全由 Apple 的 StoreKit 处理。我们不会从中得到关于你的任何信息——姓名、Apple 账号、支付信息，都不会。

AI 改一改（可选，1.2 起）。当你选择使用「AI 改一改」，你圈住的那一块画——如果你选了 「整张」模式，则是整张画——连同你写的一句提示，会传到我们的服务器，交给 AI 图像模型重画。服务器把它 转给模型提供方，把结果送回你的设备，自己什么都不留：这张图不存储、不和你关联、不用于训练，也不用于 任何别的用途。请求里带一个随机的按安装生成的标识，只用来限制每台装机每天的改稿次数，不和你的姓名、 Apple 账号或设备身份挂钩。第一次上传前 app 会先告诉你这些；不点「改一改」，它永远不会联网。除此之外 ——你的画、画廊、进度——都和以前一样只在你的设备上。

儿童

本 app 适合儿童使用。因为它完全不收集数据，所以也不会收集儿童的数据。app 内没有聊天、 没有用户之间的内容、没有外部链接，也没有广告。

分享作品

分享按钮把一张图片交给 iOS 的系统分享面板，由你决定发到哪里，之后遵循你所选 app 的规则。 简笔画家本身不上传任何东西。

政策变更

如果本政策有变更，新版本会发布在同一地址并标注新的日期。
